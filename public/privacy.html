Step Coach Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Step Coach is a web app for Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses that shows your daily step count, a goal, and a streak. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

What Step Coach uses

  • Motion sensor readings from your glasses, only while tracking is started, to estimate your steps. The raw readings are processed on the device and immediately discarded.
  • Your daily step totals, goal, and chosen screen corner, saved in your browser's local storage on the device so your streak and settings are there next time. The last 60 days are kept; older days are deleted automatically.

What Step Coach does not do

  • It does not send your steps, motion data, or any other information to a server, to the developer, or to any third party.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking cookies.
  • It does not use the camera, microphone, or your location.

Hosting

The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) when the app is loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Remove Step Coach from your glasses, or clear the site data for this app in your browser settings. That deletes everything Step Coach stored.

Children

Step Coach does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Branrulz/step-coach/issues.
