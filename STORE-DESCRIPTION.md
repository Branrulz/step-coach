## App name

Step Coach

## Short description (Developer Center project field, 100-character limit)

Hit your daily step goal. See your steps, streak, and a nudge to keep moving at a glance.

## Description

Step Coach puts your daily step goal right in your line of sight. Glance to see today's steps, a completion ring filling toward your goal, and your streak. Get a quick message when you're close to beating yesterday, and a celebration at every 25% milestone. Steps are estimated from your head's motion and saved only on your glasses.

## Long (store-style)

Step Coach is a heads-up motivator for your daily walk.

- Today's steps with a completion ring that fills toward your daily goal and turns green when you hit it
- Streak tracking: see how many days in a row you've hit your goal
- Smart nudges: "526 steps to beat yesterday," "Past halfway. Keep the pace up."
- Milestone pop-ups at 25%, 50%, 75% and goal
- Pinch to start or pause; pinch, hold and drag to move it to any corner of the display

Steps are estimated from your head's motion and saved only on your glasses. Nothing is sent anywhere.
